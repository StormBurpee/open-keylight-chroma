# Artwork

The identity follows the light it casts: charcoal, walnut, warm ivory, amber and restrained cyan. No product illustration is used as a substitute for a photograph of the actual hardware.

The banner and Stream Deck key artwork were generated with OpenAI's built-in image generator under the art direction recorded in `art-direction.json`. They are illustrations, not photographs or evidence of measured light output. The open and closed lock variants share the same body. The plugin exports proportional raster sizes for its actual display surfaces.

Project artwork is distributed under the repository's Apache-2.0 license to the extent applicable. No Razer logos or vendor artwork are included.
